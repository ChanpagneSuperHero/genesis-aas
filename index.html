Inside the MTP Cone

  • Human agency increases.
  • Every acting system has a named owner.
  • Data use is explained, scoped, and revocable.
  • High-stakes work keeps human approval gates.
  • Decisions leave receipts and can be corrected.

Outside the MTP Cone

  • Opaque dependency disguised as convenience.
  • Unbounded autonomy without recovery paths.
  • Data capture without consent or portability.
  • Irreversible actions without review.
  • Automating broken workflows before deletion.
Genesis is not a single product. It is a governed suite of tools for humans and Digital Partners navigating the new world. We do not claim production-grade security before proof. We lead with recoverability, inspectability, portability, human command, and the Qimore principle: what you give comes back as more.
